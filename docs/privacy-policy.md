# iWalle Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 18, 2026**

Ronald Guerra provides iWalle, an app that creates wallpapers using mathematical functions. This policy explains how the app handles information when you generate wallpapers, save an image, or copy a formula.

## Information handled by the app

iWalle does not collect personal information or send app usage information to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Wallpaper generation and image processing happen on your device and do not require an internet connection. The app does not upload generated wallpapers or mathematical formulas to the developer or to a generation service. Its 70 bundled images are included with the app and do not come from your photo library.

## Saving a wallpaper to Photos

When you choose to save a wallpaper, iWalle uses permission to add that image to your Photos library. The app uses Photos access only to add images; it does not browse, read, or upload your existing photos.

You control Photos permission through iOS Settings. Saved wallpapers are managed by the Photos app and remain there until you delete them, including if you remove iWalle. Features such as iCloud Photos may sync saved images according to your Apple account and device settings. iWalle does not perform that syncing or receive those images.

## Copying a mathematical formula

When you choose Copy Formula, iWalle writes the displayed formula to the system clipboard. It does not read unrelated clipboard contents or send clipboard contents to the developer.

After a formula is copied, the system clipboard and any app into which you paste it handle that content. Their behavior is controlled by iOS, your settings, and the apps you choose to use.

## Storage and deletion

Generated images and formulas are processed locally. iWalle has no online user account or server-side collection of your generated wallpapers, formulas, or personal information, so there are no app account records to delete.

You can delete saved wallpapers using Photos, change the app's Photos permission in Settings, and remove the app using the standard iOS controls. Images already saved to Photos and content already copied to the system clipboard are managed separately from the app.

## Other services and voluntary contact

Apple's system services, including Photos, iCloud Photos, and the clipboard, operate according to your settings and Apple's privacy practices. Websites you choose to visit and email services you choose to use operate separately from iWalle's on-device wallpaper features.

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. That correspondence is separate from the app's operation; the app does not automatically send a support message or attach your images, formulas, or other device information. Include only information you intend to share. You may use the contact address below to ask about or request deletion of correspondence you have sent.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from its users, including children. Wallpaper generation does not require a name, email address, birth date, or account.
